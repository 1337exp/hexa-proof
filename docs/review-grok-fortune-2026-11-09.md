SUMMARY: 448 passages compared (64 hexagrams × verdict + 6 lines, bottom line = line 1). 13 findings. The three places already marked as mended (62 verdict, 40.2, 64.5) now match the Chinese and are not listed. On fortune words the Russian pack is mostly faithful: the stock formulas (吉 / 凶 / 无咎 / 貞吉 / 貞凶 / 貞吝 / 悔亡 / 元吉 / 大吉) usually keep their valence, and Shchutsky’s paraphrase is not the problem. It is not safe to trust unread. The misses are real reversals or dropped verdicts, not style: one 征吉 is printed as misfortune, one “do not persist forever” is printed as a command to be eternally firm, and several 吉, 厲, and 利 are simply absent. Where Russian and English differ on a clear Chinese formula, Legge is the one that matches. I found no passage where the English alone reverses a clear Chinese fortune word.

RU vs EN disagreements not settled:
[hexagram 10][line 5] 貞厲. Russian: “Будь стойким в опасности.” English: though firm and correct, there will be peril. Both name danger. Whether 貞 is a command or the cause of the danger is a real split; his other 貞厲 lines say “стойкость ужасна”, which matches Legge, so this one line is the odd one. Not sure it is a full reversal.
[hexagram 16][line 6] 成有渝，无咎. Russian: “В становлении будет чрезмерность. [Но] беды не будет.” English: if he changes course even when it is completed, there will be no error. The “no blame” agrees. Whether 有渝 is “excess” or “a change” I will not force.
[hexagram 33][line 4] 小人否. Russian: “Ничтожному человеку — несчастье” (his word for 凶). English: a small man cannot attain to this. 否 is “not” or “obstruction”, not the character 凶. Both deny the small man the good outcome. Not sure the Russian word is a true reversal.
[hexagram 36][line 3] 不可疾貞. Russian: “Нельзя болеть о стойкости.” English: he should not be eager to make all correct at once. I lean to Legge (疾 = in haste). “Болеть о” could be defended as “don’t be anxious about firmness”, so this is not a clean opposite.
[hexagram 47][line 6] 曰動悔。有悔，征吉. Russian: movement leads to repentance, there will be repentance, but the campaign brings good fortune. English: if he repents of former errors, going forward brings good fortune. Both keep 征吉. Whether 有悔 is a certain regret or the condition of the good fortune is a grammatical split. Not sure.
