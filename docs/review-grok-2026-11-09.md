FINDINGS

[B][three changing lines] `readCast` always makes the primary judgment the main text (`main: 0`). The short rule matches that. The fuller wording kept in the *Zhuzi yulei* and the *Zhouyi zhezhong* adds “the first ten govern by the inquiry (primary), the latter ten govern by the response (relating).” The usual gloss is: both judgments are read, but if the bottom line is one of the three that move, the relating judgment governs. I am not sure of that gloss down to the last case (confidence medium). The rest of the classic rule matches: 0 the primary judgment; 1 that line; 2 both lines, the upper one main; 4 the two resting lines of the relating figure, the lower one main; 5 the one resting line of the relating figure; 6 the relating judgment, and for 1 and 2 only the use of nines/sixes. Coins are fair (2 or 3), the sums are 6, 7, 8, 9 at 1/8, 3/8, 3/8, 1/8, and 6 flips to yang while 9 flips to yin. The relating figure is built correctly. Line order is bottom-up (confidence high).

[C][hexagram 20][image] the name in the image is “Kwan”; the heading and the judgment use “Kwân” (confidence high)

[C][hexagram 37][line 1] two sentences are glued: “in his household Occasion for repentance” -> “in his household. Occasion for repentance” (confidence high)

[C][hexagram 47][line 4] the comma after the line-tag is missing: “undivided shows” -> “undivided, shows” (confidence high)

[C][hexagram 52][image] “the (baties of the) position” -> “the (duties of the) position” is wrong; the word is “duties” (confidence high)

[D][hexagram 9][judgment] the canon is only the tail, “они — с нашей западной окраины.” The name and “Развитие” sit at the end of the comment, and the clouds-without-rain clause is gone. It should be the whole 小畜 judgment: small restraint, success, dense clouds and no rain from the western border (confidence high). This is not the known empty line 2.

[D][hexagram 19][judgment] the text starts in the middle, lowercase: “благоприятна стойкость. При достижении восьмой луны будет несчастье.” Missing the name and the opening (元亨): “Посещение” plus originating success, then firm correctness, then evil in the eighth month (confidence high)

[D][hexagram 51][judgment] the text starts at “ха-ха!” Missing the opening of 震: success, the thunder’s coming with fear, and only then the laugh, the shock for a hundred li, and the ladle of sacrificial wine not being dropped (confidence high)

[D][hexagram 62][judgment] the canon contradicts itself: “Не следует подыматься. Следует подыматься. Следует опускаться.” The middle sentence does not belong. 不宜上，宜下 is “do not ascend; do descend” (confidence high)

[D][hexagram 40][line 2] “Стойкость — к сожалению” -> good fortune. The line is 田獲三狐，得黃矢，貞吉, and Legge says firm correctness brings good fortune (confidence high). I am not sure the printed Shchutsky already has this sentence.

[D][hexagram 64][line 5] the line opens “Стойкость — к несчастью” and later says there will be happiness. The line is 貞吉，无悔…吉. The opening should be good fortune, as in Legge (confidence high). Same caveat about the printed book.

[D][hexagrams 30, 42, 61] the notes say the labels of 30.1, 42.1, 42.6 and 61.6 were corrected to “сильная черта”. In this pack they still say “слабая”. The lines are solid (30 = 101101, 42 = 100011, 61 = 110011), and the Russian words are the yang-line words. The override did not reach `texts/ru.json` (confidence high). Hexagram 2’s use of the sixes was split out correctly, and 29/30 are in King Wen order; those two known fixes did land.

WHAT YOU COULD NOT CHECK

I did not have a printed Legge or a printed Shchutsky beside the files, and I could not run a script over the JSON, so I did not collate all 384 lines word for word. For English I checked every name, every judgment’s opening, every image’s trigrams, and that each line’s “NINE/SIX” and its place-word (first through topmost) match the figure. A wrong word inside a line that still says the right NINE or SIX could remain. For Russian I checked all 64 names and judgments, both end-labels of every hexagram, and the full six lines of 1–5, 9, 21, 29, 30, 42, 61, 63 and 64. Middle labels of the other hexagrams were not opened one by one. Several comments end on a dangling «Книга Перемен» (29.1, 61’s judgment and lines 1–3, 63.1, 63.3, 64.4); I did not list every such cut. Hexagram 60’s “Горе ограничено” may be Shchutsky’s own wording of 苦節; I am not sure. 噬嗑 as shìkè is the modern reading; the Tang fanqie is hé. I do not count that as an error in the table.

OVERALL

I would trust the 64 figures, the trigrams, and the three-coin cast. I would trust the English pack for a reading after the six transcription fixes above. I would not trust the Russian canon in a divination app until the judgment cutter is fixed: a Zhu Xi reading of hexagram 9, 19, or 51 currently speaks a fragment. Fix that first, then make the four label overrides actually appear in the pack, then correct the two inverted “good fortune” lines and the contradictory sentence in hexagram 62.
